A working yacht base

Olbia carpet requests often happen between other operations.

Olbia is frequently used for crew movements, supplies, technical activity and owner transfers. Carpet cleaning therefore has to fit around people, equipment and a yacht schedule that may still be changing.

Fine dust and repeated movement can settle into salon routes and corridor edges after onboard work. Cabin carpets may also need attention after storage or before accommodation is reopened for owners and guests.

A clear sequence matters: finish contaminating work first, remove loose material, identify stains and then schedule the wet-cleaning stage with enough ventilation and drying time.

Olbia-specific priorities

Carpet cleaning should follow the operational phase of the yacht.

The following situations require different scopes and different timing.

01

After maintenance or contractor traffic

Carpet lanes may contain fine particulate matter, footprints and edge accumulation. Dry soil removal and a condition review should precede extraction instead of pushing debris deeper into the fibres.

02

Before owner or guest transfer

When arrival is linked to Olbia transport logistics, the completion deadline is fixed. Priority areas are selected according to visibility, use and realistic drying conditions.

03

Long-stay cabin freshness

Cabins closed for periods can develop stale textile odours even without obvious staining. Ventilation history and any humidity concern should be disclosed before treatment.

FAQ

Yacht carpet cleaning in Olbia: common questions

Should carpet cleaning happen before or after maintenance work?

Normally after dusty or contaminating work is complete. Cleaning too early can result in new traffic and debris immediately affecting the treated areas.

Can you work within a short owner-arrival window?

Feasibility depends on area size, fibre, ventilation, access and the drying deadline. Send details early enough for an honest assessment.

Can you assess corridor and stair-edge marks?

Yes. Photographs should show the full route and close-ups because edge wear, soil and permanent fibre distortion can look similar from a distance.

Do you cover Golfo Aranci from Olbia?

Requests in Golfo Aranci and nearby north-east locations can be assessed according to route, access and scheduling.
