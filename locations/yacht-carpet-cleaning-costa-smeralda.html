A dispersed premium market

One coastline, very different carpet-cleaning conditions.

Costa Smeralda is not a single marina. Yachts may be located in Porto Cervo, Portisco, Poltu Quatu, Cala di Volpe or nearby berths, each with different access windows, crew activity and turnaround pressure.

Carpets in this market are frequently light coloured, closely fitted and visually important. A mark in a salon or cabin can be small in size but disproportionate in the way it affects the perceived standard of the interior.

The service is therefore planned around the actual yacht, not around a generic regional package. Photographs, material information, area measurements and the required completion time shape the proposal.

Costa Smeralda carpet scenarios

Cleaning priorities change with use, material and guest schedule.

Three recurring situations require different decisions before equipment is brought on board.

01

Charter turnover and presentation

When one guest period follows another, visible routes and salon carpets may need targeted work inside a narrow operational window. The scope must match the available drying time, not merely the wish list.

02

Delicate pale carpets

Natural fibres, pale colours and previous spot treatments can create uneven results if approached aggressively. Material, backing and existing colour variation are reviewed before treatment.

03

Multiple decks and separated areas

Large yachts may have carpeted spaces across salons, cabins and different levels. Area-by-area photographs and approximate dimensions make the first assessment substantially more accurate.

FAQ

Yacht carpet cleaning in Costa Smeralda: common questions

Can you cover different Costa Smeralda marinas?

Yes, subject to route, access and timing. The exact berth must be provided because Costa Smeralda requests can involve significantly different travel and access conditions.

Can the work be completed between guest periods?

Potentially, when the scope and drying conditions fit the available window. Photos, area sizes and the fixed deadline are needed before confirming feasibility.

Do you treat single stains on luxury carpets?

Stains can be assessed, but removal cannot be guaranteed without considering fibre, age, previous products and colour change. Local treatment may also leave a visibly cleaner patch on an otherwise used carpet.

Can upholstery and mattresses be included?

Yes. They are quoted as separate textile categories so the scope, timing and priorities remain clear.
