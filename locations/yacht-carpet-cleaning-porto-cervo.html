High-visibility interiors

Carpet condition is part of the yacht's first impression.

In Porto Cervo, yacht interiors are often prepared for owner arrivals, charter guests, brokers and high-season marina stays. Carpet areas can quietly undermine that presentation when traffic lanes, dull patches or cabin marks remain visible.

Pale and natural-fibre carpets require a measured approach. The useful starting point is not a generic promise but a review of the material, backing, access, ventilation, stain history and realistic drying window.

The service focuses on the textile floor areas that matter on board, with practical scheduling around crew operations and a clear photo-based assessment before the visit.

Porto Cervo carpet priorities

Different yacht areas create different cleaning problems.

The assessment is organised by area and use, rather than treating every carpet as the same anonymous square metre.

01

Salon carpets and presentation areas

Main salons show contrast changes, compressed traffic paths and marks around seating quickly. Cleaning is planned to improve the overall visual consistency without disrupting adjoining upholstery and furniture.

02

Cabins and low-ventilation zones

Cabin carpets may hold stale odours or localised humidity concerns even when they look acceptable in a wide photograph. Close-ups and information about ventilation help define a safer scope.

03

Entrances, corridors and transitions

High-use routes collect fine dust, salt-related residues and repeated footwear traffic. These areas often need focused extraction and careful edge work rather than a superficial whole-floor pass.

FAQ

Yacht carpet cleaning in Porto Cervo: common questions

Can you assess wool or pale yacht carpets?

Yes. Send photos and any known material information. Natural fibres, pale colours, backing construction and previous stains all affect the recommended method and the result that can realistically be expected.

Can isolated stains be treated without cleaning every carpet?

Sometimes. The stain type, age, previous products and surrounding colour determine whether local treatment is sensible or whether a broader area should be cleaned for visual consistency.

How much drying time is needed?

Drying depends on fibre, carpet construction, ventilation, humidity and the amount of cleaning required. The service window is assessed before scheduling so the carpet is not treated immediately before it must be used.

Do you cover locations close to Porto Cervo?

Requests are assessed for nearby Costa Smeralda berths, including Liscia di Vacca, Cala di Volpe and other accessible yacht locations, subject to timing and operational access.
