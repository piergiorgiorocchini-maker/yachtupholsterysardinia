Specialist upholstery care

Guest-facing seating carries much of the yacht’s visible standard.

Salon sofas, cushions and dining seating collect dry soil, body oils, cosmetic residues, food and drink marks, sunscreen traces and odour over time.

Cleaning decisions depend on fibre, colour stability, construction, previous treatment, permanent damage and the available drying window. Pale or delicate fabrics deserve assessment before moisture is introduced.

The scope can be limited to priority seating or coordinated with carpet and cabin mattress work when the yacht schedule allows it.

01

Fixed seating

Main salon sofas and built-in seating are assessed for fabric, condition, edges, access and realistic drying time.

02

Loose cushions

Loose and decorative cushions are identified separately because filling, trims and construction may require different handling.

03

Visible stains

Removal is assessed honestly because age, previous chemicals, heat and colour loss can make some changes permanent.

FAQ

Yacht upholstery cleaning in Sardinia: common questions

Can upholstery stains be assessed from photos?

Yes. Send wide images, close-ups, yacht location and timing for a useful first assessment.

Can every stain be removed?

No responsible service can guarantee every stain. Fibre, age, previous treatment, colour loss and permanent damage determine what can improve.

Can cushions and fixed seating be cleaned together?

Yes, subject to assessment. They are still identified separately because construction and drying conditions may differ.

Do you also clean yacht carpets and mattresses?

Yes. These can be quoted separately or included in a combined superyacht interior textile scope.
