Carpet is not a secondary surface

It connects every visible part of the yacht interior.

Carpets carry traffic between entrances, salons, cabins and service routes. They collect dry soil, fine sand, dust, spills and odours while also showing wear and colour differences that cleaning cannot always reverse.

Effective yacht carpet care begins by separating removable contamination from fibre damage, fading, compression, backing problems and active moisture. That assessment protects the material and creates a more honest quotation.

Where appropriate, controlled extraction can remove suspended soil and treatment residues. The method, moisture level and drying plan are adapted to the carpet construction, ventilation and operational window.

What the service covers

Cleaning decisions based on material, use and condition.

Three distinct parts of the work determine the result more than a generic machine-cleaning promise.

01

Dry-soil and traffic-lane removal

Detailed preparation addresses loose particulate in open areas, edges and transition zones before moisture is introduced. This is particularly important for dense pile and carpets affected by fine sand or contractor traffic.

02

Stain and spot assessment

Food, drinks, cosmetics, tracked residues and unknown previous products require different treatment. Removal is assessed realistically because age, heat, fibre and prior attempts can permanently alter a stain.

03

Odour, humidity and drying review

Textile cleaning may improve odours held in the carpet, but active leaks, condensation or damp material beneath the surface must be addressed separately. Ventilation and return-to-use time are part of the plan.

01

Natural and wool carpets

Natural fibres and pale carpets are assessed for colour stability, existing variation, backing, previous treatment and realistic moisture limits before cleaning.

02

Synthetic fitted moquette

Synthetic carpet may tolerate a different process, but density, adhesive, underlay, edge access and accumulated residues still influence the result and drying time.

03

Decorative rugs

Loose rugs are identified separately from fitted surfaces because backing, fringe, edge construction and hidden colour contrast may require a different scope.

Assessment process

Four inputs produce a useful first quotation.

1

Photographs

Send wide images of every area and close-ups of stains, traffic lanes, edges and colour differences.

2

Material and dimensions

Provide known fibre information and approximate square metres or room dimensions where possible.

3

Location and access

Identify the marina, berth, onboard contact and any clearance or equipment-access constraints.

4

Operational deadline

State when the carpet must be dry, inspected and available for owner, guest or crew use.

FAQ

Yacht carpet cleaning in Sardinia: common questions

Do you clean both fitted carpet and yacht rugs?

Yes, subject to assessment. Rugs should be identified separately because their construction and safe treatment may differ from the fitted carpet beneath them.

Can all yacht carpet stains be removed?

No responsible service can guarantee every stain. Fibre, age, previous chemicals, heat, colour loss and permanent damage determine what can improve.

Do you clean wool yacht carpets?

Wool and other natural fibres can be assessed. The method depends on construction, colour stability, existing wear, backing and available drying conditions.
