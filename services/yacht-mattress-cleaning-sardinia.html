Cabin textile care

A prepared cabin should feel as clean as it looks.

Mattresses are largely hidden once a cabin is dressed, but they still affect freshness, comfort and the perceived standard of the accommodation.

The service is intended for yacht owner, guest and crew cabins before intensive seasonal use, owner arrival, guest boarding or reopening after a long period of limited ventilation.

Mattress condition, surrounding humidity, access, fabric construction and drying time are assessed before the scope is confirmed.

Combined interior scope

Mattresses can be coordinated with carpet and upholstery work.

For larger yacht and superyacht projects, cabin mattresses may be included in a defined interior textile scope alongside salon upholstery, cushions and carpets, subject to workload, access and drying conditions.

FAQ

Yacht mattress cleaning in Sardinia: common questions

Can multiple cabin mattresses be assessed from photos?

Yes. Send wide images, cabin count, approximate dimensions where available, location and timing.

Can mattresses be treated during the same visit as carpets or upholstery?

Yes, when the total workload, ventilation and available drying window make a combined service realistic.

Do you work in crew cabins as well as guest cabins?

Yes. Owner, guest and crew mattresses can be included in the assessed scope.

Can every odour be resolved by mattress cleaning?

No. Active moisture, cabin ventilation, surrounding materials and the source of the odour must also be considered.
