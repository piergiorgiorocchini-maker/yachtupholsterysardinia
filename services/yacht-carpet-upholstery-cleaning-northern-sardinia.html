Material and condition assessment

The visible mark is only part of the diagnosis.

Fibres, dyes, backing, pile condition, humidity, previous chemicals and permanent wear can change both the correct method and the realistic result.

Photographs and on-board inspection help distinguish removable contamination from fading, abrasion, dye loss, pile distortion or structural damage.

Operational reference

Typical drying windows

Carpets and fitted carpets treated with controlled extraction typically require about 4–8 hours; upholstery and cushions about 6–12 hours. Wool, thick padding, high humidity or limited ventilation can extend drying to 12–24 hours.

Practical questions

Useful answers before requesting the service.

Work, access, materials, drying and payment are clarified before scheduling, so the service can be organised around the yacht's actual requirements.

How much does yacht carpet and upholstery cleaning cost?

As an indicative reference, limited interventions are often around €400–€600, medium multi-area work around €1,200–€2,000, and larger yacht or superyacht textile projects around €2,000–€5,000 or more. The final quotation depends on scope, material, access and timing.

What photographs and information should I send?

Send wide views of each area, close-ups of visible marks, the yacht or marina location, the number of areas to be treated and the required timing. Material details or manufacturer information are useful when available.

Can you work while the yacht is in berth?

Yes, subject to berth access, equipment movement, power availability, crew activity, ventilation and the agreed drying window. These points are confirmed before scheduling.

Can carpets, upholstery and mattresses be treated in one visit?

Often yes. A combined service is practical when access, ventilation, service sequence and return-to-use requirements allow it.

How long will treated areas take to dry?

As an operating reference, carpets and fitted carpets treated with controlled extraction typically require about 4–8 hours; upholstery and cushions about 6–12 hours. Wool, thick padding, high humidity or limited ventilation can extend drying to 12–24 hours.

Can every stain or visible mark be removed?

No. Results depend on contamination, fibre, dye stability, age, previous treatments and permanent wear or damage. The objective is the best safe and realistic result for the material.

Do you assess wool, viscose and other delicate fibres?

Yes. Sensitive textiles require identification, colour-stability consideration and a conservative treatment plan. The assessment determines whether the requested result can be pursued safely.

What access, power or water is required on board?

Requirements depend on the work and equipment. Access routes, electrical supply, water availability, equipment position and crew coordination are confirmed before the appointment.

Can urgent or short-notice work be considered?

Short-notice requests can be assessed according to yacht location, current schedule, work required, berth access and the available drying window. Send the timing requirement with the photographs.

How are quotations and payment handled?

The quotation is agreed before scheduling. Payment arrangements are confirmed with the service details and may include bank transfer, card payment or a secure payment link. Apple Pay and Google Pay are available where supported by the selected payment channel.

Which Northern Sardinian locations do you cover?

Priority areas include Porto Cervo, Costa Smeralda, Portisco, Olbia, Porto Rotondo, Poltu Quatu, Cannigione, Golfo Aranci and Palau. Other locations are assessed according to timing and access.
