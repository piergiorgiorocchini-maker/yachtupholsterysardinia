A specialist vertical

Built for yacht interiors, not generic cleaning.

NuvolaCleaning has years of hands-on experience in upholstery, mattress and textile cleaning in Sardinia. Yacht Upholstery Sardinia was created as a focused service for yacht and superyacht interiors, where timing, access, materials and presentation matter.

The work centres on the textile surfaces guests notice first: salon seating, cushions, carpets, fitted moquette, selected rugs and cabin mattresses.

Every request begins with photographs, yacht location, required areas and timing. The objective is a useful first assessment before anyone commits to a visit or an unrealistic promise.

The specialist

Mr. Rocchini

Piergiorgio Rocchini, publicly referenced as Mr. Rocchini, is the owner of NuvolaCleaning and the direct specialist behind Yacht Upholstery Sardinia.

He personally reviews photographs, assesses the requested carpet, upholstery or mattress scope, plans access and timing, and remains the client contact throughout the service.

The focus is not generic interior cleaning. It is professional textile care for premium marine interiors, including stain assessment, pale and natural-fibre carpets, upholstery, cushions, cabin mattresses, humidity-related concerns and return-to-use planning.
