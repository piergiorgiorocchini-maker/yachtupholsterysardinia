Specialist interior textile care

The textiles guests see and use define the first impression on board.

Yacht interiors are judged quickly. Guests notice upholstery, cushions, carpets, cabin mattresses, textile freshness and visible marks long before anyone explains how busy the season has been.

Yacht Upholstery Sardinia is built for premium marine interiors, not generic cleaning dressed up with nautical vocabulary. The focus is practical: cleaner textile surfaces, better presentation and realistic operational planning.

Send photos by WhatsApp with location, timing and the visible textile issue. Mr. Rocchini personally reviews the request and replies with a practical first assessment.

FAQ

Yacht Upholstery Sardinia: common questions

Do you work on superyachts as well as smaller yachts?

Yes. The scope is assessed from the surfaces, access, timing and operational requirements rather than from a generic size label.

Can I send photos before booking?

Yes. Photos, location, required areas and timing are the preferred first step for a personalised WhatsApp assessment.

Can carpets, upholstery and mattresses be cleaned during one visit?

Yes, when access, workload, ventilation and the available drying window make a combined scope realistic.

Which areas of Sardinia do you cover?

Priority coverage includes Porto Cervo, Costa Smeralda, Olbia, Portisco, Porto Rotondo and Cagliari, with other locations assessed individually.
